AUC = share of (known analog, negative control) pairs where the known analog scores higher. 1.00 is perfect, 0.50 is chance. Matching only what matters for each test separates Mars-like from non-Mars-like sites better than matching everything.

Mission briefs

The AUC per mission shows how well that mission's scoring separates known analogs from non-Mars-like controls. Missions that use only some features (landing looks at terrain) can score lower by design: on terrain alone, flat farmland resembles flat Mars plains.
