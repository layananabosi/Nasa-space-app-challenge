What the score means: it ranks Earth sites against each other for this mission and Mars site, and it is not a percentage of similarity to Mars. On each feature, the Earth place closest to the Mars value gets 100 and the farthest gets 0; the score is the mission's weighted average of those. How to read this brief

The score ranks Earth sites against each other for this target and mission, using only the properties that matter for the test. The range shows how much it moves when the weights change. The Gap Index is the distance that remains in absolute terms: no place on Earth is Mars.
